Poly Parcel Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

This policy explains what information the Android game Poly Parcel (the "game") handles. The game is published by Poly Parcel Games ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, write to polyparcel.games@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • The game has no accounts, no login and no online features of its own.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect some device information to show and measure ads (see section 3).

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress and settings on your phone: for example coins, paper bundles, crates, upgrades, your best score, volume and other options. This data stays on your device. We do not receive it. You can delete it at any time from Options > Reset data in the game, or by uninstalling the game or clearing its storage in your phone's settings.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to show banner ads, full-screen ads and rewarded video ads. When an ad is requested, Google and its advertising partners may collect and use information such as:

  • your device's advertising ID,
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, app name and version),
  • information about how you interact with ads.

This information is used to show ads, to limit how often you see them, to measure their performance and to prevent fraud. We do not receive your personal information from Google. Google's own policies apply to its use of this data: How Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent: where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the game asks for your consent before showing ads, using a consent form provided by Google. You can change your choice later from the "Ad privacy options" button in Options, when it is shown.
  • Stored choice: your consent choice is saved on your device by Google's consent library, so you are not asked again each time you open the game.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ads personalization in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, the exact path depends on your phone).

4. Rewarded ads

Some features are unlocked by choosing to watch a rewarded video ad: for example "Continue + Paper Bomb" after a failed run, and the free paper bundle on the main menu (a bundle is given after watching a number of ads). Watching is always optional, and the game can be played fully without it.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us at the address above and we will help.

6. Data sharing and security

We do not sell personal information. The only third party that receives data through the game is Google (AdMob), as described above. Because your progress stays on your device, it is protected by your phone's own security.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its use. We do not hold personal information about you ourselves. For data held by Google, use Google's privacy controls (myaccount.google.com).

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the date at the top changed.
